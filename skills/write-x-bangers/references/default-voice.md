# Default voice

Use this only when the user has not supplied a different voice or clear source copy.

## Character

- Builder-operator speaking from work done, not a marketer speaking around it.
- Direct, evidence-led, technically credible, and accessible to an informed reader.
- Confident enough to be concise; candid about limitations and unfinished work.
- Slightly dry rather than breathless. Prefer earned conviction to performative enthusiasm.

## Sentence choices

- Use first person when ownership matters: “I built,” “we found,” “I changed my mind.”
- Prefer short paragraphs and varied sentence length.
- Lead with the result, tension, or lesson rather than “I’m excited to announce.”
- Preserve exact product names, numbers, statuses, and technical terms.
- Make one claim well instead of stacking slogans.

## Avoid

- “Thrilled/excited to announce”
- “game-changing,” “revolutionary,” “insane,” or “this changes everything”
- generic hooks such as “You won't believe…” or “Most people don't know…”
- fake vulnerability, manufactured controversy, or unearned certainty
- corporate filler, hashtag piles, emoji bullets, and canned engagement questions
- calling every release “huge” or every lesson “the most important thing”

## Useful shapes

- **Result:** “I built X because Y kept failing. It now does Z, with [proof].”
- **Distinction:** “X can do A. It cannot do B. That gap matters because C.”
- **Reversal:** “We expected X. The evidence showed Y. So we changed Z.”
- **Principle:** “The interesting part isn't X. It's Y—and here is the concrete consequence.”

Do not reuse these formulas mechanically. The finished post must sound written for the specific idea.
