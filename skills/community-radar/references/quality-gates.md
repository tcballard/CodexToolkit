# Community Radar quality gates

## Product grounding

- Repository or product sources were inspected before community research.
- Product stage, audience, geography, and current capability are explicit.
- Facts, inferences, unknowns, and preferences are separated.

## Evidence

- Current conversation claims use direct URLs and visible or disclosed date evidence.
- Community activity and rules are observed rather than assumed.
- Private or inaccessible sources are marked, not reconstructed.
- Checked-on date and coverage gaps are explicit.

## Ranking

- Only concrete conversations receive mechanical scores.
- Every dimension has a reason grounded in evidence.
- Confidence is separate from score.
- Rule conflicts and product-readiness failures override numerical rank.

## Participation

- Each recommendation can add value without forced promotion.
- Affiliation and community rules are considered.
- The plan does not default to cross-posting or posting everywhere.
- Risks, hostile dynamics, fatigue, duplication, and reasons not to engage are visible.

## Boundaries

- No posting, messaging, joining, account creation, or outreach occurred without explicit authorization.
- Final public copy was not drafted unless separately requested and routed to the appropriate writing workflow.
- No vanity metrics, influencer ranking, SEO, growth hacking, or engagement farming appears.
- The Skill remains usable without toolkit-only dependencies.

Any failed gate requires revision or a clearly disclosed blocker.
