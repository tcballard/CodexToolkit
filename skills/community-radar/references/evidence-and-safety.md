# Evidence and participation contract

## Evidence states

- **Confirmed:** observed in the inspected product source or a directly opened current community source.
- **Inferred:** reasoned from named evidence; never phrased as a source fact.
- **Unverified:** plausible but inaccessible, undated, ambiguous, or not checked.
- **Preference:** supplied by the user.

Every material audience, community, and conversation claim needs one of these states. Record `checked on` separately from a source's publication or activity date.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer:

1. repository instructions, product code, releases, issues, discussions, and maintained docs;
2. platform-native conversation pages with visible dates and authorship context;
3. official community rules, FAQs, moderator posts, and submission guidance;
4. current search results used to locate a primary page;
5. third-party summaries only when the primary source is unavailable and the limitation is explicit.

Do not cite a search-results page when a direct conversation URL exists. Paraphrase conversation content; quote only the minimum needed and respect source limits.

## Freshness

Freshness depends on the action:

- replying usually needs activity measured in hours or days;
- a persistent unanswered technical question may remain useful for weeks;
- community norms and rules require current official evidence;
- repeated problems may use older sources when recurrence is demonstrated by newer examples.

An undated page cannot support a `reply today` recommendation. Record the limitation instead of inventing recency.

## Access and privacy

Public search cannot prove activity inside private Slack, Discord, forum, or workspace channels. Use authenticated access only when the user has authorized it and the tool is available. Summarize private material minimally and never expose member identities or content outside the authorized task.

## Authentic participation test

Before recommending engagement, answer:

1. Can the participant add value if the product name and link are removed?
2. Is affiliation disclosed where relevant?
3. Do community rules permit the proposed behavior?
4. Is the conversation current enough for the posture?
5. Does the product actually satisfy the evidenced need today?
6. Would a regular member reasonably view the contribution as useful rather than inserted promotion?

If any answer is `no` or unknown, reduce the posture to observe, answer without promoting, wait, or do not engage.

## Prohibited tactics

Never recommend sockpuppets, undisclosed self-promotion, mass cross-posting, coordinated voting, scraped private identities, automated replies, engagement bait, fake testimonials, manufactured questions, or repeated posting after removal.
