# Quality rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2 before returning copy. Revise if the total is below 10/14 or any hard failure applies.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Buried or generic | Clear but soft | News or answer lands immediately |
| Facts | Vague or invented | Accurate but thin | Specific, accurate, and well chosen |
| User impact | Missing | Implied | Immediate and concrete |
| Candour | Evasive or overconfident | Neutral | Clear about state, limits, or uncertainty |
| Energy | Flat or forced | Serviceable | Human, warm, and lightly alive |
| Concision | Bloated | Mostly tight | Every line earns its place |
| Action | Generic call to action | Useful but broad | Focused next step, question, or clean stop |

## Hard failures

- Invents a fact, metric, date, commitment, authority, or product state.
- Claims to be or speak for a person or organisation without supplied authority.
- Closely copies a public post or relies on a recognisable signature phrase.
- Uses corporate filler, sustained hype, or humour that trivialises an incident.
- Adds an explanation or several variants when the user requested only finished copy.
