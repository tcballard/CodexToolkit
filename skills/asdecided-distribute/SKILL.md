# AsDecided Distribute

Distribute one strong idea faithfully. Do not turn one article into a content farm.

The approved canonical page is the source of truth. Every derivative must preserve its claims, qualifications, entity names, dates, links, and uncertainty. Read the source, its evidence ledger, and its Agent Citation Scorecard before drafting.

## Establish the distribution contract

Confirm:

- canonical title and URL;
- approval or publication state;
- primary page job and direct answer;
- target reader and triggering question;
- original contribution;
- claims allowed for reuse;
- claims that remain blocked or qualified;
- preferred founder or brand voice;
- relevant release, repository, documentation, or product context;
- destinations actually requested or justified.

Stop if the source is not approved, the canonical URL is unknown when a link is required, or a material claim lacks support.

## Choose the smallest useful asset set

Default to no more than:

1. one X thread or one standalone X post;
2. one LinkedIn post;
3. one repository, documentation, or worked-example derivative;
4. one optional visual, diagram, demo, or short-video brief;
5. one optional launch or newsletter excerpt when a real destination exists.

Do not create every format by default. Select channels based on the audience and the job of the source page.

## Transform, do not duplicate

Each derivative must be native to its destination:

- **X:** one sharp idea, concrete mechanism, or short sequence; avoid mini-blog repetition and fake suspense.
- **LinkedIn:** practical context, argument, trade-off, and consequence; avoid generic leadership language.
- **Repository/docs:** executable example, decision record, configuration, test, or implementation pattern derived from the article.
- **Visual/diagram:** clarify a mechanism, comparison, lifecycle, or failure mode that is genuinely easier to understand visually.
- **Video:** demonstrate a concrete workflow or explain one mechanism; do not narrate the article verbatim.

Every asset should add a useful angle while remaining subordinate to the canonical page.

## Preserve claim integrity

For each asset, maintain a derivative ledger:

- source passage or claim;
- transformation made;
- citation or canonical link retained;
- qualifications preserved;
- any omitted context that would make reuse unsafe.

Never introduce new benchmarks, customer claims, market claims, product capabilities, quotations, competitor assertions, or dates. Route any genuinely new idea back through `$asdecided-publishing` before distribution.

## Founder-led boundary

When writing for Tom's personal account, make the posture explicit and candid: what was built, observed, decided, learned, or remains uncertain. Do not impersonate personal experience that is not present in the source evidence. The owner must approve personal posts before publication.

Use `$merge-and-tell` for the final public copy when available. This Skill owns source fidelity, asset selection, and distribution packaging; `$merge-and-tell` owns concise public voice.

## Community boundary

This Skill may prepare a useful answer outline when a canonical page directly resolves a known question, but it must not:

- automatically post or reply;
- manufacture relevance to insert a product link;
- syndicate the same reply across communities;
- disguise affiliation;
- create reciprocal link arrangements;
- recommend posting where rules or norms conflict.

Use `$community-radar` to decide whether a conversation is appropriate. Human approval remains mandatory for participation.

## Produce the distribution package

Return or persist:

1. canonical-source summary;
2. selected channels and why each fits;
3. finished derivative assets;
4. derivative claim ledger;
5. links, media, and formatting requirements;
6. suggested posting order and dependencies, not fabricated optimal times;
7. explicit approval gates per destination;
8. assets deliberately omitted and why;
9. measurement tags or URLs needed after publication.

## Quality gates

The package is not ready when:

- the canonical source is unapproved or ambiguous;
- an asset adds a claim absent from the approved source;
- a qualification, date, or limitation is lost;
- every channel receives effectively the same copy;
- the asset is merely promotional and offers no useful idea;
- the canonical link is forced where it does not help the reader;
- a personal post implies experience or belief not supported by the owner;
- community posting or account changes are treated as implicitly authorized;
- the quantity of assets exceeds the evidence or importance of the source.

## Integration boundaries

- `$asdecided-publishing` owns the canonical source, evidence, scorecard, and refresh state.
- `$merge-and-tell` owns final public product communication.
- `$community-radar` owns community fit and participation posture.
- `$launch-pack` owns coordinated release distribution.
- `$authored-frontend-design`, `$visual-asset-adapter`, and specialist video workflows own final visual production.

All integrations are optional. The Skill must remain useful when extracted from the toolkit.

## Safety boundary

Never publish, schedule, reply, message, upload, or modify an account without explicit authorization. Never fabricate engagement, attribution, citations, testimonials, or platform performance. Never produce spammy permutations, doorway content, undisclosed sponsored material, or manipulative link placements.
