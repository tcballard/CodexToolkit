# Launch delivery matrix

Select only channels that serve the launch objective. A complete pack is not one that fills every row; it is one that deliberately includes or omits each relevant surface.

| Surface | Primary job | Typical deliverable | Specialist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repository | Explain the release to evaluators and developers | README section, release notes, upgrade notes | Launch Pack |
| Website | Establish promise, proof, and action | Landing page or release page | Authored Frontend Design |
| Product demo | Show one credible transformation | Video, captions, contact sheet, QA report | Product Demo |
| X | Land one sharp piece of news | Single post or requested thread | Merge & Tell |
| LinkedIn | Add context for a broader professional audience | Concise contextual post | Merge & Tell |
| Hacker News | Explain mechanism, trade-offs, and limitations | Submission title and first comment | Merge & Tell |
| Community | Reach existing users where they already are | Forum, Discord, Slack, or email draft | Merge & Tell |
| Store or marketplace | Satisfy structured listing and review rules | Description, screenshots, privacy/support links | Launch Pack |
| Competition or submission | Match judging criteria with evidence | Form answers, demo, provenance, checklist | Launch Pack + Product Demo |
| Press or partners | Provide reusable verified facts | Brief, asset links, approved descriptions | Launch Pack |

## Channel row contract

Every included row in `CHANNEL_MATRIX.md` should name:

- audience and desired action;
- deliverable and format constraint;
- claim IDs used;
- required assets and links;
- specialist or owner;
- output path;
- status;
- publication authority;
- blocker or next action.

## Narrative sequence

Across channels, preserve this order unless the surface imposes another:

1. What changed.
2. What a user can now do.
3. Visible or measurable proof.
4. Important boundary or limitation.
5. Where and how to try it.

Vary depth and cadence by channel without changing the underlying facts.
