# Quality gates

## Evidence

- A real source opens and its SHA-256 is recorded.
- Source priority and permitted changes are explicit.
- Target macOS/Xcode/distribution context is known.
- Version-sensitive claims have current official Apple URLs and a checked date.
- Facts, asset defects, craft judgments, and unknowns use distinct labels.

## Inspection

- Dimensions, square geometry, format, alpha, bounds, and four edge clearances are measured.
- Likely baked mask, external halo/shadow, and internal shading are assessed separately.
- Full-size artwork is visually inspected.
- A labeled representative-size grid is inspected on contrasting backgrounds.
- Heuristics are not presented as platform compliance.

## Preparation

- The user authorized editing and its permitted class.
- The change is deterministic and non-semantic.
- Central subject identity, geometry, and useful depth are preserved.
- No platform mask is baked without a current explicit requirement and justification.
- Inseparable raster shells route to specialist editing rather than fake cleanup.
- Source remains unchanged unless replacement was explicitly authorized.

## Handoff

- Prepared source meets its declared dimensions/format/alpha contract.
- Before/after hashes and transform parameters are reported.
- Design, source/export, integration, runtime, and release statuses are separate.
- Remaining Xcode/Icon Composer work is named.
- Signing, packaging, notarization, and App Store readiness are not implied.
