# Premonition dev-log entry template

Write a short diary entry only after the paired BUILDLOG entry is factually settled. Use `merge-and-tell` in Draft mode when available.

```markdown
## <matching-entry-id> — <human, specific title>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

<Three to seven short paragraphs explaining what changed, why it mattered,
what was surprising or unresolved, and what happens next. Use only facts from
the paired BUILDLOG entry.>

**Source:** `BUILDLOG.md`, Entry <matching-entry-id>
```

Prefer candour over ceremony. Say “the app still does not exist” when that is the useful truth. Do not turn ordinary progress into a launch announcement, invent tension, or imply that a plan has shipped.
