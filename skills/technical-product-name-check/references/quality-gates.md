# Technical naming quality gates

## Context

- Product, category, audience, distribution plan, and candidate spelling are explicit.
- Repository evidence was inspected before asking for discoverable facts.
- Required namespaces are distinguished from optional research.

## Current evidence

- Every checked result has a date, direct source, query/variant, and result state.
- Universal checks ran for every candidate.
- Category-specific coverage matches actual distribution plans.
- Material results were opened and classified, not judged from snippets alone.
- Blocked, incomplete, ambiguous, and not-checked sources remain visible.

## Reasoning

- Exact, near, same-category, inactive, unrelated, command, and search collisions are separated.
- `Not found` is not described as available, reserved, clear, or legally safe.
- Package, command, repository, display, and domain names are evaluated separately where needed.
- The risk rating maps to evidence and uses no false numerical precision.
- Suggested variants solve named problems rather than restarting ideation.

## Report

- Recommendation, namespace plan, caveats, and not-checked section are explicit.
- A re-run identifies what changed.
- Legal/trademark limits are stated clearly.
- No purchase, reservation, creation, publication, or outreach occurred without authority.

Any failed gate requires revision or a `Blocked` result.
