# Review standard

## Contents

- Review loop
- Hard gates
- Editorial review
- Frame sampling
- Reporting

## Review loop

1. Render a review copy using final dimensions and frame rate.
2. Validate technical metadata.
3. Extract a contact sheet and targeted frames.
4. Watch once without stopping for comprehension and pacing.
5. Watch again against the claims ledger and shot list.
6. Review audio and captions separately.
7. Fix the highest-impact failure first and repeat affected checks.
8. Render the final export only after hard gates pass.

## Hard gates

The export must:

- satisfy the platform's duration, dimension, frame-rate, codec, and size limits;
- open and play from start to finish;
- contain the intended audio streams or be intentionally silent;
- make every required product action and result legible;
- contain no exposed secrets, personal data, private notifications, or unrelated account details;
- keep all material claims verified or visibly qualified;
- contain accurate captions synchronized closely enough to preserve meaning;
- avoid clipped titles, unsafe caption placement, black frames, frozen frames, and accidental cursor artifacts;
- identify material time compression when it affects interpretation;
- end cleanly rather than on an accidental application frame.

## Editorial review

Ask:

- Can a viewer explain the product after the first complete workflow?
- Does the meaningful result appear early enough?
- Is every explanatory sequence earning its time?
- Does motion direct attention or merely decorate the frame?
- Are cuts happening after comprehension rather than immediately after action?
- Is the product's native visual character still recognizable?
- Is the voiceover describing what matters rather than narrating every click?
- Could one sentence, shot, or animation be removed without losing meaning?
- Does the close state one memorable promise and one next action?

## Frame sampling

Use a uniform contact sheet to catch global problems, then inspect targeted frames at:

- first visible frame;
- first product frame;
- each application-state transition;
- every title and caption style;
- each zoom or crop transition;
- any edit joining separate takes;
- the final visible frame.

Frame inspection supplements full playback; it does not replace it.

## Reporting

Classify findings:

- **Blocker:** false claim, privacy leak, corrupt export, missing required proof, or platform rejection risk.
- **Major:** confusing workflow, unreadable product state, broken audio, or seriously mistimed captions.
- **Minor:** pacing, alignment, polish, or non-material consistency issue.
- **Observation:** optional improvement with no acceptance impact.

For every blocker or major finding, include a timestamp, evidence, smallest credible correction, and required recheck.
