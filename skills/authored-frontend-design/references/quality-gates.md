# Frontend quality gates

Reject and revise the output if any gate fails.

## Coherence

- One clear narrative governs typography, color, motion, grid, material, iconography, and copy.
- The signature element is functional and inseparable from the product idea.
- Components are conceptually connected rather than polished in isolation.
- Responsive transformations preserve hierarchy and rhythm.

## Completeness

- Semantic tokens own styling decisions.
- All applicable interaction, data, network, validation, and recovery states exist.
- Copy is contextual and deliberate; no lorem ipsum, vague claims, placeholder labels, or silent failures remain.
- The requested scope runs without relying on default framework or browser failure presentation.

## Accessibility and performance

- Native semantics come first.
- Keyboard operation, visible focus, meaningful reading order, WCAG AA intent, and non-color status cues are verified.
- Reduced motion is complete rather than a partially broken afterthought.
- Costly visuals initialize lazily and have a reduced mode.

## Anti-convergence

Reject generic rounded-card SaaS composition, Inter plus purple gradients, stock chatbot bubbles, default Tailwind or component-library appearance, marketplace-template resemblance, ornamental animation, invented filler copy, and custom polish that removes standard usability.
